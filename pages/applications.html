APPLICATIONS OF NAIVE BAYES

Discover how the Naive Bayes algorithm is used in various real-world applications.

Spam Detection

Naive Bayes can classify emails or messages as spam or not spam by analyzing the words and patterns contained in the text.

Sentiment Analysis

Naive Bayes can identify whether a review or opinion expresses positive or negative sentiment based on its words.

News Classification

Naive Bayes can categorize news articles into topics such as sports, technology, politics, and business.

Language Detection

Naive Bayes can predict the language of a text by analyzing the frequency and patterns of characters or words.
